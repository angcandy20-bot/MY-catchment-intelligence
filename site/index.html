Methodology

  • Population: GHS-POP cells are kept at their original resolution. A cell counts when its centre falls inside the polygon (holes excluded, MultiPolygon parts counted once). Values are people per cell, so they are summed; densities are never summed. NoData and zero cells are excluded.
  • Wealth matching: each population cell is matched to its nearest valid RWI sample by great-circle distance in metres. Matches farther than the threshold are not used. Unmatched cells still count toward total population but not toward RWI.
  • Population-weighted RWI = Σ(matched cell population × RWI) ÷ Σ(matched cell population). Wealth coverage = matched population ÷ total population. With no valid matches the dashboard shows “No data”, never zero.
  • Area is geodesic on the WGS84 ellipsoid; population density = population ÷ area (people per km²).
  • RWI measures relative wealth within the country (an index centred near 0). It is not household income or spending in ringgit.
  • The population and RWI datasets refer to different years (see Datasets above).
  • The RWI error field is a per-sample uncertainty. It cannot simply be summed (or averaged) into a confidence interval for a region, so no regional interval is shown.
  • Walking distance areas come from HeiGIT openrouteservice (foot-walking profile, range_type: distance, default 200 m) and follow OpenStreetMap footways, streets and crossings from the building point. Accuracy depends on the point being at the building (ideally its entrance) and on how completely footpaths are mapped in OpenStreetMap. Batch output has one row per building.
  • Driving isochrones come from HeiGIT openrouteservice (driving-car, OpenStreetMap roads). They are modelled travel times without real-time traffic. Each minute’s contour is the cumulative area reachable within that time, so populations of different minutes must not be added together.
